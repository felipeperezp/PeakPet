THE IDEA THAT STARTED IT ALL

What do an elite athlete and a pet seeking a healthier life have in common?

More than you might think: individual goals, nutrition, assessment, follow-up and a constant pursuit of wellbeing.

PEAKPET was born where veterinary science, academic excellence and a high-performance mindset meet.

Dr. Felipe Andrés Pérez Pezoa’s extensive professional and academic background, his international experience in animal nutrition, and a life dedicated to competitive sport inspired a belief: wellbeing and performance benefit from knowledge, objective measures and continued follow-up.

A veterinarian, Doctor of Business Administration (DBA), Master in Business Administration (MBA), and holder of a master’s degree in Sales Management and Marketing, Felipe is currently pursuing postgraduate/master’s studies in Small Animal Nutrition. His continuing education connects science, strategy and constant improvement.

After helping develop veterinary initiatives including Centro Veterinario Dr. Argandoña, ALANA and THERAVET, he created PEAKPET: a concept that brings the high-performance approach to personalized nutritional recommendations for dogs and cats.

Every pet has different needs, but all deserve the opportunity to become their best selves.

SPORT · SCIENCE · DISCIPLINE

From the BMX track to precision nutrition

For more than three decades, Felipe has lived high-performance sport from the inside: training, competing, measuring progress, adapting and trying again. World Championship finals, national titles and podium finishes in the United States have shaped his journey.

That experience inspires a simple, powerful idea: progress is not an accident. It takes preparation, nutrition, consistency and informed decisions. At PEAKPET, this mindset becomes an individualized approach to each animal’s wellbeing.

EDUCATION · INTERNATIONAL EXPERIENCE · TEACHING

Knowledge that evolves. Projects with purpose.

His career combines veterinary medicine, regional experience in animal nutrition, advanced education and postgraduate teaching. His current studies in small animal nutrition deepen the perspective behind PEAKPET.

His goal is not just to gather knowledge but to apply it: understand each individual case, turn evidence into practical recommendations and keep learning to offer better support.
