01 / WHY ASSESS?

Weight alone doesn’t tell the whole story.
We need to understand what each pet needs.

One portion size does not fit every pet. We assess the factors that support informed feeding decisions tailored to health and lifestyle.

01 / BODY

Body condition

Weight and body condition score (BCS), together with a professional examination.

02 / MUSCLE

Muscle condition

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers before you begin.

Does my pet need to play sports?

No. PEAKPET assesses companion dogs and cats, pets needing weight management, and active or athletic animals.

Will you recommend a specific pet food brand?

We review the current diet and suitable commercial foods or professionally formulated diets. Recommendations depend on the pet’s history, needs and professional assessment.

Are photos enough to assess body condition?

No. Photos can support recordkeeping, but body and muscle condition should be confirmed through a professional assessment and physical examination when appropriate.

Does this replace my regular veterinarian?

No. Nutrition advice complements regular veterinary care. Some patients need tests, in-person examinations or referral.
